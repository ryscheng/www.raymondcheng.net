# Artio.so

<img src="/img/projects/artio.svg" alt="Artio" width="80" />

Artio is the intelligence engine for ambitious organizations. It unifies every signal into one trusted, AI-native data platform, so humans and agents collaborate over the same source of truth and turn raw data into live intelligence. Scattered sources converge into one semantic layer, collapsing time-to-insight from months to minutes, with infinitely customizable interfaces that give stakeholders exactly the views they need.
