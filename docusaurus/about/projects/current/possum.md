# POSSUM

**P**rosperous **O**pen **S**ource **S**oftware **U**niverse **M**aintenance is a project of the Public Goods Foundation to secure open source software at AI speed and scale. AI has democratized and accelerated the ability to find and exploit vulnerabilities, and maintainers can't absorb the flood of reports. POSSUM is a public good that patches open source, ships fixes through a secure registry, and notifies the vulnerable, one package ecosystem at a time: Linux, C/C++, npm, Python, Ruby, and more.
