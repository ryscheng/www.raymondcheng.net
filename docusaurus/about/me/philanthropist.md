# Philanthropist

> Once men turned their thinking over to machines in the hope that this would set them free. But that only permitted other men with machines to enslave them. *- Frank Herbert, Dune*

I co-founded and serve as President of two non-profits that invest in the shared infrastructure our digital lives depend on.

## Public Goods Foundation

<img src="/img/philanthropist/pgf-logo.png" alt="Public Goods Foundation" width="160" />

**President** · 501(c)(6) non-profit

Public goods are the rising tide that lifts all boats. They are the shared infrastructure every company, lab, and community builds on, and that none of them owns alone. Over 70% of all software is open source, and public goods provide trillions of dollars in economic value per year. The Public Goods Foundation is a neutral hub for public goods advocacy, with a mission to cultivate sustainable funding models that ensure public goods can thrive for generations to come.

### Security

**[POSSUM](https://www.prosperous.software/en/possum)** (Prosperous Open Source Software Universe Maintenance) is a coordinated effort to secure and maintain the open source software the world runs on, one package ecosystem at a time: Linux, C/C++, npm, Python, Ruby, and more.

## MASQ Foundation

<img src="/img/projects/masq.png" alt="MASQ Foundation" width="128" />

**President** · 501(c)(3) non-profit · *Machines and Security Quest*

Building a more secure and private future for all. The MASQ Foundation supports security, privacy, and decentralization for the public good. We research, engineer, and promote widely-accessible technologies that empower individual users and local communities.

Too much of our world has been concentrated into powerful centralized infrastructure, and whoever controls that infrastructure holds unilateral power over the products that shape our lives. Every person deserves a private personal space where they can be true to themselves, think freely, communicate safely with friends and family, and act independently. With cryptography, self-custody, and decentralized protocols, we can give that power back to the individual, and build a digital world on public goods and open source software that puts people and their rights first.

Our programs include:

- Grants for developers who build open source technologies
- Fiscal sponsorship for mission-aligned non-profit projects
- Education, including classes, conferences, and documentation
