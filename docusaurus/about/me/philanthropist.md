## Public Goods Foundation

<img src="/img/philanthropist/pgf-logo.png" alt="Public Goods Foundation" width="160" />

The Public Goods Foundation is a 501(c)(6) non-profit and a neutral hub for public goods advocacy. Our mission is to cultivate sustainable funding models that ensure public goods, starting with open source software, can thrive for generations to come. Projects include [POSSUM](/about/projects/current/possum), [Open Source Observer](/about/projects/current/oso), the [Prosperous Software Movement](/about/projects/current/prosperous), [Funding the Commons](https://fundingthecommons.io/), and the [Open Source Scholarship](https://www.publicgoods.foundation/scholarship/).

## MASQ Foundation

<img src="/img/projects/masq.png" alt="MASQ Foundation" width="128" />

The MASQ Foundation (Machines and Security Quest) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit that supports security, privacy, and decentralization for the public good. We research, engineer, and promote widely-accessible technologies that empower individual users and local communities, through grants for open source developers, fiscal sponsorship for mission-aligned projects, and educational programs.

- President
